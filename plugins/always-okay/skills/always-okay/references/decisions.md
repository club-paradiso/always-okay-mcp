# Decision casebook

Purpose: 12 decisions from Min Hee-jin's publicly documented professional work, each shown in its context, for the always-okay lens (method, not persona; an analyst's reading, never her voice).
Use the reasoning, never the look: transfer why a choice fit its situation, not the colours, objects, styling or formats it produced.
Pick a case by matching the situation (attention level, what is still unknown, constraint, who executes), not by genre, category or surface similarity.
These are documented examples, not rules; principles stay defaults (see the principles), and a case never settles a user's case on its own.
Built only from paraphrased notes in src/always_okay_mcp/data/notes.json (note IDs → source IDs); a field the notes do not cover reads Not documented.
Credit follows tensions.json (CX-08..CX-22): shared work is named as shared, self-accounts are labelled as such, and years are source dates where the notes give none.

**Context-dependent sets (적용 조건).** The same principle, applied differently because the situation differed. Read the set together; the stable part is the reason, the variable part is the format.
- Launch sequencing, P21/P22: D05 (13 teasers in a day, 2014) · D07 (no teasers, video first, 2022) · D12 (23 timed videos with no prior notice, 2026). Reconciled in CX-36.
- Friction calibration, P13: D04 (a slight unfriendliness, 2014) · D11 (moves anyone can follow, 2023). Reconciled in CX-33.

### D01 A healthy image instead of decoration (Girls' Generation debut, era E1; year not in notes)
- Situation: A new girl group was launching; on hearing the group name she made an image map and briefed the producer on the cover concept and overall visual direction.
- Constraint: She was not yet in overall charge; her remit widened step by step afterwards, with stage costumes coming later.
- Options on the table: The default she found boring, decorating singers prettily, against a distinct image proposal.
- Choice: A healthy, refreshing image meant to stand out in a harsh entertainment environment and appeal to men and women alike; the cover took its motif from distinct sisters living in harmony (a literary source).
- Stated or documented reason: The crux was what kind of girl, and how girlhood would be expressed; when what you want to show is clear, the story follows and can be left to viewers' imagination.
- What it shows: Interrogating the category word ("girl group") and positioning by contrast with the surrounding house style, rather than polishing the default (P02, P05).
- Transfer note: Take the move of asking what the stock category word should mean here and defining it against what the environment already does. Do not take the motif, the palette or a "healthy" look as a default.
- Evidence: MHJ-EV-00184 → MHJ-SRC-000202; MHJ-EV-00017 → MHJ-SRC-000002. Credit: proposals made before she held overall charge; later 2009–2011 releases credit a chief visual director above her (CX-08, CX-19).

### D02 A symbol to make a premise credible (EXO debut period; sources 2013–2014)
- Situation: A boy group launched on a premise of superpowered beings from an unknown planet.
- Constraint: The premise was hard to make credible; its author is not established in the record (one low-confidence note attributes it to the producer).
- Options on the table: Her general default of not favouring a single symbol or logo-work for albums, against a dedicated symbol for this group.
- Choice: A group symbol built from basic geometric shapes with planet and meteor motifs, applicable to accessories, merchandise and stage costumes, persisting across albums but varied per release (e.g. kiss as a heart, hug as a circle).
- Stated or documented reason: A symbol embodying the concept would organise the story and lend persuasiveness; varying it instead of repeating it would let the story grow over time.
- What it shows: Fixity is chosen where a mark does structural work, and a fixed core can still vary in application (P17, P10).
- 적용 조건: Symbol adopted because the premise needed help to be believed; compare D08, where the reason was the absence of any unifying mark.
- Transfer note: Take the test of whether a mark does a structural job for this project before making one. Do not take the planetary motif or the idea that every launch needs a logo system.
- Evidence: MHJ-EV-00186 → MHJ-SRC-000202; MHJ-EV-00509 → MHJ-SRC-000262; MHJ-EV-00164 → MHJ-SRC-000195; MHJ-EV-00508 (low) → MHJ-SRC-000262. Credit: symbol system rests on her account and design press; premise authorship open (CX-16).

### D03 The second-best route that harms no one (f(x) Pink Tape art film, 2013)
- Situation: She had a plan for what f(x) should be from the start, but its realisation had fallen short.
- Constraint: She could not replace people; as a first attempt the film could not have a large budget (reportedly about KRW 5 million).
- Options on the table: Replacing people was not available; beyond that, Not documented.
- Choice: A fun, ultra-low-budget side project: an art film she proposed, planned and styled, directed and shot by a film-department student whose coursework she had seen, joined by fellow students; the album's symbolic object set its title and design.
- Stated or documented reason: The aim was to set an image standard for the group, not technical craft; budget is not proportional to result; she needed a director who understood the aim and cared about the work more than money.
- What it shows: When the main route is blocked, a small, harmless proof can reset a standard (P29, P32, P26).
- Transfer note: Take the logic of a cheap side proof that changes the reference point without displacing anyone. Do not take the format (art film), the object or the colour.
- Evidence: MHJ-EV-00030, -00029 → MHJ-SRC-000005; MHJ-EV-00499, -00503, -00498 → MHJ-SRC-000262; MHJ-EV-00481 → MHJ-SRC-000356; MHJ-EV-00032 (medium) → MHJ-SRC-000004. Credit: planning and styling hers; direction and filming by 민제홍, 정우성 and 이성은; budget figure is reported, not verified (CX-17).

### D04 Calibrated friction for a differentiated act (f(x) Red Light, 2014)
- Situation: An idol group needing mass appeal amid what she saw as a flood of predictable groups.
- Constraint: Mass appeal was required; she also worried that teenage pop listeners met only a narrow culture.
- Options on the table: Not documented as discrete options; she names two poles to avoid, too difficult and (worse) too easy and light.
- Choice: Expand the lyric's warning into opposed codes in one frame (taboo and longing, utopia and dystopia), leaving metaphors and symbols for fans to decode, while stopping short of the point where the public finds it too unfriendly.
- Stated or documented reason: Easy things tire quickly; the real risk for such a group is the boredom of uniformity; meaningful work with a slight unfriendliness makes people think.
- What it shows: Difficulty is a dial set by the act's purpose, held between accessibility and the centre that must be kept (P13, P09, P05).
- 적용 조건: Higher friction suited an established act whose job was differentiation; compare D11, where the job was easy uptake by anyone. The stable element is an easy-to-enter surface with depth on decoding (CX-33).
- Transfer note: Take the deliberate setting of how much effort the audience is asked to spend, and why. Do not take the opposed-codes imagery or treat obscurity as depth.
- Evidence: MHJ-EV-00037, -00035 → MHJ-SRC-000008; MHJ-EV-00169, -00173 → MHJ-SRC-000200; MHJ-EV-00023 → MHJ-SRC-000002.

### D05 Thirteen teasers in a day (f(x) Red Light, 2014)
- Situation: The album's concept had many metaphors that she wanted to keep feeding to an audience already watching.
- Constraint: Many parts of the concept could not easily be explained to another photographer.
- Options on the table: Not documented.
- Choice: She photographed the teasers and album herself and released thirteen teasers between 9 a.m. and 9 p.m., numbers people quietly treat as taboo; each image's moment, order and intended small reaction were calculated.
- Stated or documented reason: Keep throwing metaphors without letting anticipation drop, so immersion and understanding rise together and viewers hear the music with eyes and talk as well as ears; shooting herself let her judge coverage on set.
- What it shows: The teaser is a sequencing instrument whose density follows the concept and the audience's state (P21, P22, P06).
- 적용 조건: Dense teasers fit an established act with an attentive audience and a metaphor-rich concept; compare D07 (unknown act, high attention, no teasers) and D12 (no artists yet, timed fragments).
- Transfer note: Take the rule that each reveal needs a reason for its moment and position. Do not take the count, the time window or the numerology.
- Evidence: MHJ-EV-00170, -00171, -00172 → MHJ-SRC-000200. Credit: photography is her self-account; f(x) work generally involved others (CX-19).

### D06 Space that forms attitude (HYBE rebrand and Yongsan headquarters, design 2019–2020, presented 2021)
- Situation: A company rebrand extended into a move to new headquarters, with teams forming per project.
- Constraint: Fixed placement was judged inefficient for project teams; the building was a group design with outside architects.
- Options on the table: Furniture: a wholly new design against customising an existing industrial system; customising was chosen (taken together with her, per the architects).
- Choice: Treat the move as a main axis of the brand system, not a relocation: free seating with lockers and reservations, reconfigurable racks and walls, identical floor structures for easy orientation, and function-led lighting and ceiling choices; an identity presented as a staff line designed to change and extend.
- Stated or documented reason: Space elicits action, so space should change first; people should come together of their own accord rather than be placed together; good branding contains the process of forming the attitude you want.
- What it shows: The concept is the whole lived experience, and identity rigidity follows how much the organisation must change (P06, P17, P19).
- Transfer note: Take the question of what behaviour the environment should make easy, and design function first. Do not take the open plan, the rack grid or the staff-line mark.
- Evidence: MHJ-EV-00203, -00213 → MHJ-SRC-000062; MHJ-EV-00312, -00314, -00311, -00309 → MHJ-SRC-000111; MHJ-EV-00513, -00514, -00515, -00516, -00517 → MHJ-SRC-000226; MHJ-EV-00520 → MHJ-SRC-000059. Credit: she oversaw the rebrand as CBO and was one member of a Spatial Experience Design Group; the architects claim the grid and racks; the presentation lists a credited team and outside studios; logo authorship is unresolved (CX-14, CX-43).

### D07 No teasers; the video first (NewJeans debut, 2022)
- Situation: A debut with members unrevealed and even their number unknown, while attention was already high.
- Constraint: A rookie with no fan base needed a fandom quickly; the new concept was the music itself, unconventional for idol music and needing time to be absorbed.
- Options on the table: The usual teaser, then video, then audio sequence, against releasing music videos first.
- Choice: No teasers; music videos released first, carrying three title songs; member names withheld and each member styled very differently per video so viewers would rewatch and search.
- Stated or documented reason: Teasers had become a custom and were unnecessary with attention already high; the intended feeling would not come across in stills; peak first-view attention should be converted into listening; concealment raises curiosity.
- What it shows: A category reflex is listed and dropped for a stated reason, and the first encounter is chosen by which sense carries the feeling (P16, P21, P22).
- 적용 조건: No teasers fit an unknown act with high existing attention and a sound-led concept; the second EP then led with a teaser for its last track, so teasers returned when they served a planned reaction (CX-36).
- Transfer note: Take the test of whether a ritual step still does its job given current attention, and lead with the medium that carries the feeling. Do not take no-teaser launches as a style.
- Evidence: MHJ-EV-00218 → MHJ-SRC-000085; MHJ-EV-00234, -00235 → MHJ-SRC-000222; MHJ-EV-00531, -00533 → MHJ-SRC-000269.

### D08 A mark that is also a toy (NewJeans light stick; source 2024, release year not in notes)
- Situation: The group had no logo but needed a unifying symbol.
- Constraint: Not documented.
- Options on the table: Not documented.
- Choice: By her account, the letters N and J resolved into a bunny face; the large head reads as a heart, and removable eyes let each fan customise their own.
- Stated or documented reason: The members resembled bunnies, bunnies multiply (a hope for a growing fanbase), so the face stands for both members and fans.
- What it shows: A symbol drawn from what the project already has, built so the holder can do something with it (P03, P17, P23).
- 적용 조건: Symbol adopted because there was no mark to unify members and fans, and given a customisable part; compare D02, where the job was making a premise credible.
- Transfer note: Take the derivation from the project's own letters and people, and the built-in act of customising. Do not take the animal, the shape or a mascot by default.
- Evidence: MHJ-EV-00542 → MHJ-SRC-000269.

### D09 How much to brief depends on who executes (NewJeans Ditto video and Hype Boy choreography, sources 2023; SM stylists for contrast)
- Situation: A release meant to look completely different from the debut, made with a director who had never made an idol video; separately, a choreographer stuck after a first full draft.
- Constraint: The director hesitated to take on an idol video; his formally radical approach could have been a burden from a producer's standpoint.
- Options on the table: For the chorus, the draft's triangle formation against a looser alternative (documented); for the video, Not documented.
- Choice: For the director, purpose talk (a horizontal fan-idol relationship, the release as a gift) plus one optional image she pictured on hearing the song, then full authority over scenario and direction, and meals and practice-room visits so he knew the members. For the choreographer, a call saying she could take her time, that the chorus need not be a triangle, and suggesting an easier take on a current style.
- Stated or documented reason: Not to constrain the director's thinking (his account); for the choreography, keep it unboxed and unpredictable while still mass-appealing (Kim's account).
- What it shows: Brief the reason behind the impression, and correct only the point a specialist missed (P24, P25, P26).
- 적용 조건: At SM, for stylists, she explained the target concept and ordered the core anchor items, then developed their production plan back and forth; for a director with his own method, one optional image; for a stuck choreographer, one pinpoint note. Depth of input follows the medium and how far the concept depends on specific items.
- Transfer note: Take the practice of matching briefing depth to the collaborator and giving access to the subject instead of references. Do not take the images, the props or the narrative devices.

### D10 The device makes the work (NewJeans ETA x Apple, 2023)
- Situation: A phone maker proposed a collaboration.
- Constraint: The partner's brief was to showcase a camera feature.
- Options on the table: The partner's first proposal, a choreography video showcasing the feature, against shooting the main music video on the phone; for the track, a fast hard-hitting song against a flash-mob song or a mood piece.
- Choice: By her account she counter-proposed shooting the main video on the phone, matched to the fast track; choreography was built from the outset around phone filming, with the members free to improvise the ending each stage.
- Stated or documented reason: The act of filming with the device should carry meaning rather than merely highlight a feature; camera movement shows best on a fast track; phones suit how the group's generation hangs out.
- What it shows: The object's real use carries the idea, and a partner brief is reframed so both sides gain (P19, P23, P02).
- Transfer note: Take the move from showing a feature to making the feature the means of the work. Do not take phone-shot video as an aesthetic.
- Evidence: MHJ-EV-00228, -00229, -00230 → MHJ-SRC-000108; MHJ-EV-00242 → MHJ-SRC-000265; MHJ-EV-00527 → MHJ-SRC-000419. Credit: the counter-proposal is her self-account; the scenario and its reasoning are the director's (CX-12).

### D11 Easy moves anyone can follow (NewJeans Super Shy, Get Up EP, 2023)
- Situation: A second EP after a debut that had invited people into the group's world.
- Constraint: Not documented.
- Options on the table: Not documented.
- Choice: On first hearing she pictured many people in one space repeating the same moves; she asked the choreography director for easy moves anyone could follow, imagined as a flash mob; the EP was sequenced so tracks one to six felt connected, framed as symmetrical to the debut EP.
- Stated or documented reason: The repetitive beat, simple rising structure and shy theme suggested addictive repetition; the first EP invited people in, this one sends the group out into the world.
- What it shows: The material's first impression sets the friction level, and depth moves into structure (the paired EPs) while the surface stays easy (P07, P13, P10).
- 적용 조건: Low friction suited a song built on repetition and a goal of spreading outward; compare D04, where friction served differentiation. Both keep an easy entry and a second layer.
- Transfer note: Take the derivation of difficulty from the material and the placing of depth in structure. Do not take flash-mob choreography or symmetry as a template.
- Evidence: MHJ-EV-00232 → MHJ-SRC-000108; MHJ-EV-00241 → MHJ-SRC-000265. Credit: the moves were made by the choreography director she briefed (compare CX-13).

### D12 A launch as a timed exhibition of fragments (ooak records launch, 2026)
- Situation: A new label opening before it had any artist to show.
- Constraint: The business blueprint was still vague (her account); a first boy group was to be recruited.
- Options on the table: Not documented beyond the label's stated contrast with the industry's typical one-way promotion.
- Choice: Channels opened without prior notice; a date-only post and apparel printed with the launch time preceded any explanation; 23 campaign videos at ten-minute intervals (about 3h50m) placed a record shop in street scenes of many cities, ending in Seoul, speaking in fiction fragments, with recruitment notices and clues to the boy group hidden as easter eggs; a worldwide audition poster reused a face glimpsed in the videos.
- Stated or documented reason: Give a sense of direction while the blueprint is vague, using imagery of childhood record shops that sold their own goods; short bursts keep the public looking for answers.
- What it shows: When there is nothing yet to show, the withholding itself becomes something to do, and recruitment is folded into the reveal (P21, P22, P23).
- 적용 조건: Timed fragments fit a launch with no artists yet and a recruitment need; compare D05 (dense teasers for an established act) and D07 (no teasers for an unknown act). In each, the sequence was designed for a specific reaction (CX-36).
- Transfer note: Take the matching of reveal format to what exists and what the audience can act on. Do not take the record-shop scene, the interval count or easter eggs as a launch default; withholding must reward and never hide terms or access information.
